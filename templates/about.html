What I focus on

Most of my work sits at the intersection of machine learning and backend engineering. I don’t stop at a notebook — I design the data flow, train and compare models, then wire the result into a real Flask/Supabase service with storage, auth-aware admin tools, and a public UI.

Examples: a job-market analytics pipeline (live Adzuna data → cleaning → Supabase → salary model), house-price models compared across Ridge, XGBoost, LightGBM and Gradient Boosting, and this portfolio itself — a production app with an admin CMS and a Groq-powered assistant.

How I work

I start from the problem, not the tool. Need fast lookups? Use the structure that fits — BST, queues, sets — because they’re right for the job, not because a lab brief asked for them. For ML, I measure: cross-validation, honest baselines, and metrics that mean something (RMSLE, not vanity accuracy).

I’m NAVTTC-certified in AI & Machine Learning and finishing Saylani’s AI & Data Science program (EDA, classical ML, and deep learning fundamentals with TensorFlow). Day to day I live in Python, Git, and shipping small vertical slices end to end.
