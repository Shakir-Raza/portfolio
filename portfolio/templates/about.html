Who I am

I'm a Computer Science student from Karachi, Pakistan with a genuine passion for technology and building things that work. I started coding because I wanted to create — not just consume — and that curiosity has led me down some exciting paths.

Currently I'm focused on three areas: Full Stack Development, AI/ML, and Data Science. I believe these three together are the future of software, and I want to be ready for it.
